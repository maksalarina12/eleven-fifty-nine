## 1. Narrative canon and resolution

The sealed chamber from Part 9 opens. It contains Noah's original biological body, which Elias kept alive in suspended sleep for three years. The copied Noah has lived through repeated loops. Elias's stated aim of preserving consciousness masked an attempt to create obedient, controllable minds. Amara had concealed a shutdown protocol. When she activates it, the two Noahs briefly synchronize memories; copied Noah chooses to release the consciousnesses held in the system. The local loop ends. Three days later, Noah is missing, and Amara receives a message directing her to `PROJECT EDEN`. The final image hints at a much larger system, setting up Season 2.

**Continuity note:** Part 9's revelation that the original Noah “died” is recontextualized: the official record reported him dead, while Elias secretly maintained the original body. This is an intentional plot twist, not a silent retcon. Make the distinction clear visually in archive screens, without inventing a new medical explanation.
