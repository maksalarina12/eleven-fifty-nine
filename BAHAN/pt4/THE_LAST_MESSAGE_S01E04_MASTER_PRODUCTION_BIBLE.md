# 1. SERIES CONTEXT FOR A NEW CHAT / NEW GENERATION SESSION

This is **Episode 4** of a continuous anime-style psychological thriller. Episodes 1–3 established Noah alone in a rain-soaked apartment at 11:59 PM; an unknown message warned him not to open the door; a CCTV feed showed an identical Noah outside; the recording reached midnight while his own phone remained at 11:59; the recorded Noah collapsed, with an unidentified figure behind him; the last message read **“You have one minute left.”**

Episode 4 continues **immediately** from that message. Noah discovers his clock cannot advance past 11:59, receives **147 repeated warnings**, sees an identical version of himself standing outside in the rain, receives a call with a woman's voice, and the story reveals **Amara** watching his apartment from a remote monitor room. Do not introduce Dr. Elias in this episode. Do not reveal the full explanation of the loop yet.

The real Noah is **inside** his apartment throughout the episode. The second Noah appears **outside the window, in the rain**, for one brief reveal. The two Noahs are identical in face, hairstyle, scar and clothing. They must never be accidentally merged, duplicated in the room, or substituted for one another.
